Controls

Every key, in one place

Wordwave is played entirely from the keyboard — plus a built-in on-screen keyboard on phones and tablets. Here is the complete control map: typing, pause, settings, loadout and the way back home.

01 At a glance

Space Pause / resume
Esc Pause / resume
Ctrl Tap alone → Settings
Ctrl+H Return home
Ctrl+Alt+C Ship & weapon
A–Z 0–9 Type to attack

02 Keyboard controls

Type to attack

Type the first letter of a falling word to lock on, then keep typing. Every correct letter fires a shot; the final letter destroys the word. Letters, numbers, hyphen and apostrophe are all accepted.

Pause / resume

Press Space or Esc at any time during a run. Press Space twice quickly to return home.

Audio settings

Press and release Ctrl on its own. Holding it with another key cancels the shortcut, so it never fires by accident.

Return home

Press Ctrl+H, double-tap Space, or choose Back to Home from the pause menu.

Change ship & weapon

Press Ctrl+Alt+C to open the loadout screen from anywhere in the game.

While a word is locked

Finish it before targeting another. A wrong key breaks your streak — accuracy is scored as strictly as speed.

Shortcuts are ignored while you are typing in a text field — the custom word box and the volume sliders behave normally.

03 On-screen HUD buttons

Button Where What it does
⚙ Game HUD, right side Opens the loadout: ships and projectile animations
♪ Game HUD, right side Opens audio settings without leaving the run
Ⅱ Game HUD, far right Pauses the game and shows the pause menu

04 Touch controls

On-screen keyboard

On phones and tablets, a compact keyboard appears under the playfield. Tap a letter to type it — it contains only the characters your current word list actually needs.

HUD buttons

Tap ⚙ for loadout, ♪ for settings and Ⅱ for pause — exactly the same as on desktop.

Menus

Every menu is a full-width panel with large tap targets. No hovering, no right-click, no hidden gestures.

Rotate freely

The canvas rescales to fit your screen. Turning the device keeps the ship, HUD and keyboard in place.
