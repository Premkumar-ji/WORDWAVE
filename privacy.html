Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Wordwave has no accounts and no server of its own. Everything you do in the game stays in your browser. This page covers what is stored locally, how advertising works, and the simple rules for playing.

Last updated: 1 January 2025

PRIVACY · 01 The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or address.
  • We do not run a database, and no game data is sent to us.
  • Your settings, scores and custom word list live in your browser's localStorage.
  • The only third party on this site is Google AdSense, which may use cookies to show ads.
  • Wordwave sets no tracking cookies of its own and runs no analytics.

PRIVACY · 02 What is stored in your browser

Wordwave saves a single entry called wordwave-save-v1 in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device unless you clear it. It contains:

KeyWhat it holdsWhy
settings Volume levels, music/SFX on-off, chosen shooting sound, hit sound and background track So your audio preferences survive a refresh
stats Best score, best wave, games played, longest streak, best accuracy To power the “My stats” screen
selectedShip, selectedWeapon, shootingMethod Your loadout choices So your ship and weapon are remembered
customWords The words you typed on the “Use your own words” screen To refill the box next time you open it

To delete all of this, open your browser's site-data settings for this domain and clear storage, or run localStorage.clear() in the browser console. The game will simply start fresh.

PRIVACY · 03 Cookies and third-party advertising

This site displays advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may set cookies or read device identifiers to serve and measure ads.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info/choices.
  • For full details, read How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Wordwave itself sets no tracking cookies of its own and does not run any analytics or fingerprinting scripts. Audio and image files are served alongside the game as ordinary page assets; no personal information is attached to those requests.

PRIVACY · 04 Children's privacy

Wordwave is a general-audience typing game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — including children — because we do not collect personal information at all.

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about advertising shown on this site, you can use the opt-out links above or a browser-level ad blocker.
